# feat(dsv41): DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 + DSpark on vLLM, B200 TP4/EP4 and DP4/EP4

Draft. DP4 Phase 1 TTFT is outside its tolerance; see Test Result.

## Purpose

Model DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 served by vLLM (model runner V2) with the DSpark speculative proposer
(5 drafted tokens, verify width 6). It runs on 4x B200 in two topologies:

- TP4/EP4: tensor-parallel attention behind one scheduler.
- DP4/EP4: data-parallel attention with four schedulers. Experts are all-gathered over the DP ranks.

The work builds on the TP4/EP4 no-spec arch already on `main` (`doc/pr/dsv41-flash/`).

- **Arches.**
  - `deepseek_v41_vllm_dspark` is the TP4 target plus the DSpark draft.
  - `deepseek_v41_vllm_dp_attn_ep_moe` and `deepseek_v41_vllm_dp_attn_ep_moe_dspark` are the DP4 target, with
    and without the draft.
  - The DP4 target runs an idle rank as a dummy step at the uniform decode query length. The draft's
    all-gatherv is capped, and the dummy verify has the verify width.
  - MRV2 step shapes, the CUDA-graph capture list and the chunk are selector parameters.
- **Worklets and ops.**
  - The DSpark context-KV and Markov draft step.
  - The MoE FFN under DP/EP.
  - The mHC overlap boundary.
  - Attention:
    - write-KV-only mode;
    - a KV-only `kv_insert` on a full-width head shard;
    - `wq_b`, indexer `wq_b` and `wo_b` priced as GEMM only, because their MXFP8 input is already quantized;
    - the decode global top-k remap as its own placeholder.
  - Routed experts see only the scheduled rows.
  - The step's framework plumbing is priced as elementwise placeholders.
- **Worker.** A speculative worker selects DP placement with `dp_placement`. `vllm-least-loaded` follows
  vLLM's DP coordinator.
- **L1.**
  - `compressed_sparse_mla_rope_cast` gains the `swapab_cutedsl` decode backend.
  - `single_gemm` gains `flashinfer_mxfp8_prequantized`, the GEMM without its activation quantize.
  - `moe_ep_quantized_all_gather` accepts MXFP8 dispatch.
  - `engram_lookup` allows TP1 tables up to 400M rows.
  - Runner fixes follow the fork's MXFP8 config and its interleaved V4.1 KV records. `flashmla_mega` passes
    int64 positions and reserves the prefill workspace, as the warmup run does.
- **model.work.** A DSpark proposer accountant for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017.
- **Alignment pack** `presets/alignment/dsv41_flash_b200_dspark/` has 20 cases:
  - alignment Phase 2 is 18 cases: TP4 and DP4, each crossing chunk 2048, 4096 and 8192 with prefill-heavy,
    mixed and long-context agent workloads;
  - alignment Phase 1 is 2 cases, `tp4_diverse_100` and `dp4_diverse_100`.
  - Every case reads its calibrated inputs from its own workload pass (`from_capture`). There are per-topology
    label rules.
  - Routing reads the DSpark k5 `diverse_100` capture.
- **Presets.** Public DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash presets drop the bounded-replay what-if axis. There are no-spec and
  DSpark simulation presets for TP4 and DP4.
