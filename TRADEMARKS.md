# Trademarks and Branding

This policy covers the project's name, logo, and branding. It is a separate
concern from the software licenses, which cover copyright and patents.

> The software licenses govern copyright and patent permissions in the
> software. They do not grant permission to use project names, logos, or
> branding in a way that falsely suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or
> official status.

No registered trademark is claimed for "ServingStudio Sim" here, and the `®` symbol is
not used. This document states how the project asks that its name be used; it
does not assert rights beyond what the project can actually establish.

## Uses that are fine

Truthful, descriptive references need no permission. For example:

- "a fork of ServingStudio Sim"
- "built with ServingStudio Sim"
- "compatible with ServingStudio Sim"
- naming ServingStudio Sim in a paper, talk, benchmark, comparison, or bug report

Use the name to say what something *is* or what it *works with*. That is
nominative use and this policy does not restrict it.

## Uses to avoid

- Naming a fork or derivative in a way that reads as an official distribution.
- Using the project name or logo as the primary branding of your own product,
  service, or hosted offering.
- Any presentation implying the project sponsors, endorses, reviews, or
  certifies your work when it does not.

If your use might leave someone thinking your build is the official one, rename
it or add a clear disclaimer.

## Relationship to the software licenses

Receiving the software under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 or PolyForm Internal
Use 1.0.0 does not by itself grant any right to use the project's name or logo
as branding. Likewise, a commercial license grants the rights it states and no
implicit branding rights beyond them.

## Third-party names

Names such as vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, FlashInfer, NVIDIA, PyTorch, and the
model names appearing in this repository belong to their respective owners. They
are used here descriptively, to identify the software and models ServingStudio Sim
measures, models, or interoperates with. No affiliation or endorsement is
implied.
